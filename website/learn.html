Recovery model

Recover the root. Leave the data alone.

Application keys sit below a stable user/account root. A Recovery Deck unwraps that root; rotating the deck replaces only its wrapper.

Anti-patterns

The mistakes worth memorizing.

Most failures happen around the cryptography: serialization, lifecycle, logs, concurrency, and false trust.

×

Authorize from inspection

Keyless metadata is a preview, not authenticated truth. Route conservatively and trust only after open.

×

Compress mixed secrets

Shared compression context plus observable ciphertext length can become an oracle. Keep one trust boundary or leave compression off.

×

Rotate by nudging a deck

Nearby permutations are searchable after compromise. Secure recovery rotation starts over with a wholly fresh random deck.

×

Let two tabs own rotation

Sequential changes are detected. Truly concurrent lifecycle writers require an application-level transactional lock.
