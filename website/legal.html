Website privacy

No account. No submitted secrets.

The public site does not ask for an account, Recovery Deck, encryption key, plaintext, or protected customer data. Interactive cryptographic demonstrations execute locally in the browser and use ephemeral values that disappear when the page is refreshed or closed. Do not enter real secret material into a demonstration.

Infrastructure providers may process ordinary request metadata such as IP address, user agent, requested path, and timestamps to deliver and protect the site. Voided does not require the plaintext contents of a Recovery Deck or derived Recovery Key.

Report privately.

Send suspected vulnerabilities through a private GitHub Security Advisory. Do not include real plaintext, raw keys, Recovery Deck order, Recovery Key, stable root, customer data, or secret environment values.
